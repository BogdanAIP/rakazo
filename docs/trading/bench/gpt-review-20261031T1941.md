1. **Quantity/notional semantics:** a single 48h tranche, allocated at 0.25 gross to an asset that moves 1 → 2 → 4 across daily marks, produces gross 0.50 in the engine versus 0.75 for fixed contract quantity 0.25. At a 1% settlement and reference mark 4, the engine funding is -0.0025 versus fixed-contract -0.01. The current repeated `weight * step_return` and `weight * funding_rate` do not replicate a fixed-contract hold. If daily constant-notional rebalancing is intended instead, its quantity changes and trading costs must be explicit. Official [OKX documentation](https://www.okx.com/en-gb/help/how-to-calculate-future-funding-fee) calculates USDT-contract funding from settlement position value including mark price. No certified historical settlement marks are currently in this panel.
2. **Cash calendar gaps:** two successive ineligible decisions in the six-bar flat fixture produce steps `[0, 1, 3]` instead of all four calendar steps `[0, 1, 2, 3]`. Turnover is correct, but an all-cash interval disappears. Nominal-step annualization, folds, years and HAC therefore still need complete calendar handling. The new annual-Sharpe/HAC design addresses the prior mixed-horizon issue; it does not make omitted intervals harmless.
