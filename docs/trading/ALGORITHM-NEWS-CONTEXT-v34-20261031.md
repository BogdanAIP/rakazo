# v34: historical news evidence and availability limits

This register was assembled after the executable pilot was frozen and before
inspection of its new signal/outcome summary. It does not change the candidate,
cohort, score, exclusion rules or primary endpoint. It is selective context,
not a complete historical newsfeed or a test of news predictive value.

The frozen program annotates preceding-120-minute SCHEDULED context per alarm.
That annotation does not turn scheduled time into certified actual availability.
Daily dated pages are not assigned invented intraday times. Prior-day items are
not silently forced into the 120-minute window.

No complete original news/social-media history, publication revisions,
liquidation stream or historical open interest with calibrated availability
has been established. Each alarm therefore retains NEWS_COVERAGE_UNKNOWN.
Failure to find another headline is never evidence of no news. Timing
coincidence is not a causal explanation, and this register cannot rescue a
failed primary market test through retrospective news exclusions.
