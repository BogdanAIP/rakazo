## What the failure establishes and what it does not

The interval candidate generated 67 alarms with complete quote paths. It failed the prespecified screen on every panel: three negative means and a positive NEAR October 8 mean below two simpler controls. Zero triggers or insufficient comparator counts cannot explain this result. The exact current candidate is rejected under this development protocol; changing the fee or band width until that conclusion reverses is not a new discovery.

Conditional execution uncertainty removed only thirteen of 670 raw point-estimate signal opportunities. It removed more depth-delta opportunities but supplied no consistent incremental return. Thus further hardening of this same visibility bound is not the next justified mechanism. The two-date sample also cannot establish that all liquidity information is useless.

The current rank admission is relative: a small score can rank highly in a quiet market. Multiplying a score by a cost-corridor capacity ratio does not itself predict an absolute fee-clearing payoff. The earlier hindsight opportunity diagnostic must never be used as a live feature or an asset-selection oracle.

## Proposed new operator: matured economic-episode memory

Investigate a reviewed stateful primitive that stores outcomes only after the entire historical quote episode has finished. Its action is driven by the estimated **net quoted payoff and incremental value over a price-only control**, not the within-instrument rank of a directional score. This changes the decision target and memory semantics rather than the preceding thresholds. Cost-aware statistical decisions and queue models are established methods; the proposed construction has no established scientific novelty.

1. Fix a small state representation in advance using causal depth/flow quantities in contract and price units, their visibility-ambiguity category and past market context. Specify exact states, candidate count, normalization and all data requirements before new payload access. Keep a price-only learner with the same update rules, a constant/flow control and the prior fixed-rank rule as comparators.
2. An episode started at T has delayed entry at T+1 and no later exit than T+16 minutes. It may update memory only when all price and coverage evidence is already in the past. In the present minute framework, T+17 is a conservative earliest update time, even when a barrier was crossed earlier. Unknown paths are missing labels, not zero returns. Verify prefix invariance under arbitrary later price/print changes and purge at least the whole maturity span at split boundaries.
3. Accumulate distributions by chronological day block. The action requires both an absolute net-payoff criterion and incremental evidence relative to the price-only learner; otherwise output ABSTAIN. Do not interpret the conditional historical execution proxy as actual PnL. Declare an initial floor of at least twenty distinct training-day blocks before fitting an uncertainty-dependent admission rule; this floor does not guarantee statistical independence or adequate power in every state. Sparse states remain unpowered and cannot borrow future outcomes.
4. Predeclare dependence-aware uncertainty sensitivity, multiple-state/program selection accounting, loss-tail reporting, calendar-time abstention and hypothetical exposure before evaluation. Do not turn a model-estimated interval into a distribution-free guarantee for changing markets. Keep every model/ablation result, including cold-start zero-action outcomes.

Implement the primitive as an explicitly reviewed extension to the typed grammar. Hypothesis JSON remains data, never dynamically executed Python or other model text. Reuse verified original source replay and the existing causal feature primitives; no new LLM provider, model configuration or paid capability is required.
